Greenlit

Photograph a restaurant menu. Greenlit reads it and sorts every dish against your diets, allergies and dislikes, so you can see what to order, what to ask about, and what to skip.

Coming soon to the App Store

Three kinds of dish

  • GreenlitNothing in it conflicts with your profile, as far as the menu says. These sit at the top.
  • Ask firstFine if the kitchen leaves one thing out. Greenlit flags it and shows you what to ask them to hold.
  • SkipBuilt around something you avoid. Dimmed at the bottom, so it stays out of the way.

How it works

  1. Snap the menu. Take photos of each page, or share menu photos and PDFs into Greenlit from any app.
  2. It’s read once. AI turns the menu into dishes and ingredients, including the ones the menu doesn’t print, like fish sauce in a pad thai.
  3. Sorted on your phone. Your diets and allergies are applied on your device. Change them and every saved menu re-sorts instantly, with no rescan.

Your profile stays yours

Your diets, allergies and food preferences never leave your phone. There’s no account, no ad tracking, and no location access. The menu photos are sent off to be read and aren’t kept by us. Read the privacy policy for the details.

A guide, not a guarantee. Greenlit uses AI to read menus, so it can misread a dish or miss an ingredient, and it can’t see what happens in a kitchen, such as cross-contamination. If you have an allergy, always confirm with the restaurant before you order.
