Common questions

A dish was sorted wrongly, or the menu was misread.

Greenlit uses AI to read menus, and it can occasionally misread a dish or miss an ingredient. Email us a screenshot of the dish and we’ll investigate. For anything that matters to your health, please also confirm with the restaurant before you order.

Is Greenlit medical or allergy advice?

No. It’s a guide that helps you read a menu faster. It can’t see how a kitchen prepares food, so it can’t account for cross-contamination, and it isn’t a substitute for asking the restaurant.

Where does my diet and allergy profile go?

Nowhere. It stays on your phone, and the sorting happens on your device. When you scan a menu, only the photos are sent off to be read; your profile is never part of that request. See the privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

No. Greenlit doesn’t have accounts.

How do I delete my data?

Open Profile → Settings. Clear Scan History deletes your saved menus and the menu photos stored on your phone. Reset Profile clears your diets, allergies and preferences. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. We don’t keep your menu photos on our servers. For anything else, email us.

I got a message about a scan limit.

To keep the service running for everyone, there’s a limit on how many menus can be scanned per hour. Wait a little and try again. Menus you’ve already scanned can still be reopened.

Does it work without a connection?

Scanning a new menu needs a connection because the menu is read on our servers. Menus you’ve already scanned open from your phone and re-sort against your profile without one.
