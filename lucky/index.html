How to play
  • Most clovers have three leaves. Find the one with four.
  • Each wrong pick wilts a leaf of luck. Out of luck, and the run ends.
  • A clean find, with no misses, grows a leaf back.
  • One hint per round. Each round the meadow gets fuller, and rarer decoys turn up.
  • Daily gives you one fresh meadow a day, and a streak for every day you find it.
  • On a keyboard, the arrow keys move around the meadow and Enter picks.
